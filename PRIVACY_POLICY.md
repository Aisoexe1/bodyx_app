# Privacy Policy for BodyX

**Last updated:** July 18, 2026

BodyX ("we," "our," or "the app") is a fitness and body-composition tracking app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

**Before publishing this policy or submitting to the App Store, replace every `[BRACKETED]` placeholder below, and read the "Notes for the developer" section at the end — it flags two things that must be true in production for these claims to be accurate.**

## Information We Collect

### Account information
When you create an account, we collect your email address, a username, and a password. If you sign in with Google or Apple instead, we receive a verified identity token from Google/Apple — not your Google or Apple password — which we use to create or authenticate your account. With Sign in with Apple, you can choose to hide your real email address; we only receive whatever Apple's relay provides.

### Profile and body data
To personalize your plan, we collect the information you provide during setup and in Settings: name, gender, height, weight, age, fitness goal, activity level, and unit preference.

### Health and fitness data
If you enable Health sync, BodyX reads the following from Apple Health or Google Health Connect, with your explicit permission: steps, active calories burned, sleep stages, water intake, weight, and body-fat percentage. **BodyX only reads this data — it never writes anything back to Apple Health or Health Connect**, and it does not read heart-rate data. This data is used only to populate your dashboard and is stored on your device; it is not automatically transmitted to our servers as part of the sync itself.

### Weight, body measurements, and progress photos
Weight entries, body-fat percentage, and per-muscle-zone body measurements that you log yourself are stored on your device and, once you're signed in, synced to our servers so your data is available if you reinstall the app or sign in elsewhere.

Progress photos you capture or select are stored **only on your device**. They are never uploaded to our servers or any third party.

### Food and activity logs
Meals you log (with their calorie and macronutrient values), workouts, mobility/stretching sessions, and water intake are stored on your device to power your daily dashboard. This data is not transmitted to our servers.

### Notifications and widgets
Reminder notifications (e.g. hydration or workout reminders) are scheduled entirely on your device — BodyX does not use a push-notification service, and no notification content is sent to or generated by our servers. The same applies to the Home Screen widget and Lock Screen / Dynamic Island activity timers: they read and display data that is written locally on your device and never leave it.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:
- Create and secure your account
- Calculate your personalized plan (calorie target, water goal, protein target, etc.)
- Display your dashboard, progress charts, and history
- Sync your account, weight, and body-measurement data across sessions and devices
- Respond to support requests you send us

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal data to anyone.

## Third-Party Services

BodyX does not include any advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK. We do not share your data with data brokers or advertising networks.

The only third parties BodyX interacts with are:
- **Apple** and **Google**, when you choose to sign in with Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In (they issue an identity token to verify who you are; see their own privacy policies for how they handle that)
- **Apple Health** / **Google Health Connect**, if you enable Health sync (governed by Apple's and Google's own health-data privacy rules)
- **Google Fonts**, used to load typefaces used in the app's design; this may result in a network request to a Google-operated content delivery network

## Data Storage and Security

Your account credentials are protected with an encrypted session token stored in your device's secure credential storage (Keychain on iOS). Data synced to our servers is transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Other app data (your logs, settings, and cached profile) is stored locally on your device.

## Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Privacy → Delete Account. When you do:
- All BodyX data stored on your device — including your profile, logs, settings, and progress photos — is permanently and immediately erased.
- We send a request to delete your account and associated data from our servers.

If you simply sign out without deleting your account, your locally stored history is kept on the device so it's there if you sign back in, but your session credentials are cleared.

## Children's Privacy

BodyX is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the details below and we will delete it.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent for Health data access at any time via your device's Settings app. You can exercise most of these rights directly in BodyX (Settings → Profile, Settings → Privacy) or by contacting us.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

## Notes for the developer (delete before publishing)

This policy was drafted from a direct audit of the current BodyX codebase (commit `fe3e35e` on `aisodev`), not from a template — every claim above matches what the code actually does today, with two exceptions that need to become true before the policy is accurate for real users:

1. **"Transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection"** — today `API_BASE_URL` defaults to `http://10.0.2.2:8000/api/v1` (a local dev placeholder, plain HTTP), and no production backend is deployed at all yet (task: stand up a real hosted backend). This policy assumes that whatever backend eventually goes live serves HTTPS. Don't submit to the App Store, and don't point real users at this policy, until that's true.
2. **Account deletion "We send a request to delete your account and associated data from our servers"** — the client sends `DELETE /users/me`, and since commit `c37d54d` a failed attempt is persisted and automatically retried on later launches (the session is never restored for a deleted account). Once a real backend exists, verify server-side deletion actually completes end to end so this promise holds in practice.

Also worth knowing when filling out **App Store Connect's separate "App Privacy" (nutrition label) questionnaire** (a structured form, distinct from this document) — based on the same audit:
- Health & Fitness data: collected (steps, calories, sleep, water, weight, body-fat %), used for App Functionality, not linked to advertising, not used for tracking.
- Contact Info: email collected for account creation.
- User Content: photos (progress photos) — collected but stored locally, not on your servers, so this can be marked "not collected by developer" if Apple's definition is server-side collection, or disclosed as on-device-only depending on how you want to answer it — check Apple's current guidance since this distinction has caused rejections before.
- No advertising or analytics identifiers are used anywhere in the app (confirmed: no analytics/crash/ad SDK in `pubspec.yaml` or native project files).
